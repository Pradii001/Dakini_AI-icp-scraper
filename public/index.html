Potential clients

Scores help prioritize research; they do not confirm purchase intent. Contacts are public company channels and may not identify a decision-maker.

How is the potential-client score measured?

The score reflects evidence that Dakini AI's published customer-service, sales, and knowledge agents could help this company through multilingual support, customer-context lookup, human escalation, and system integrations. AI/voice/contact-center solution vendors are penalized as competitors. View Dakini AI's public capabilities. This is a prospecting heuristic, not a purchase probability.
