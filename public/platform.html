PROSPECT PORTFOLIO

Potential clients

Ranked by evidence of operational fit and Dakini AI relevance.

Fit scores are rule-based research priorities, not purchase probabilities. Company legitimacy labels summarize first-party website evidence; they are not legal or registry verification. Source excerpts are evidence of what a page says, not independently verified facts; directory-only entries have not had company operations checked. Suggested Dakini workflows are hypotheses to validate. Public contacts may not identify a decision-maker.

How is the potential-client score measured?

The score reflects evidence that Dakini AI's published customer-service, sales, and knowledge agents could help this company through multilingual support, customer-context lookup, human escalation, and system integrations. AI/voice/contact-center solution vendors are penalized as competitors. View Dakini AI's public capabilities. This is a prospecting heuristic, not a purchase probability.

High potential: score ≥90, configured ICP criteria match, a Dakini-aligned workflow is detected, and an explicit, non-negated buying/automation signal is present. All tiers require human review. Saved records are rescored when the server next starts; an active discovery run is not interrupted.
